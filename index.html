Frequently asked questions

Do the mockups have a watermark?

No. All downloads are free and watermark-free.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Upload your design and download the mockup instantly, no account needed.

What resolution is the download?

2400 × 2400 pixels, as PNG or JPG.

Are my designs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and your art never leaves your device.

Can I use the mockups on Etsy or Shopify?

Yes, you can use the generated images in your listings and stores.

Which design format works best?

A PNG with a transparent background. If you use a JPG, turn on the option to remove the white background.
